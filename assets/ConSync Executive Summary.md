ConSync — Construction Lifecycle Management System

## 🎯 Core Idea

**ConSync** is a **web-based Construction Lifecycle Management System** designed to integrate **Project & Cost Management** with **Supply Chain & Logistics** — addressing Nigeria’s chronic issues of project inefficiency, cost overruns, and fragmented supply chains in the construction industry.

---

## ⚙️ Theme Focus

**Combined Themes:**

1. **Project & Cost Management**
2. **Supply Chain & Logistics**

This double-theme approach ensures the platform captures both **project transparency** and **logistical efficiency** — two of the biggest bottlenecks in Nigeria’s construction ecosystem.

---

## 🧠 Problem Context (Nigeria)

The Nigerian construction industry faces:

* Poor communication and coordination among clients, contractors, and suppliers.
* Frequent **project delays**, **cost overruns**, and **abandonment**.
* **Manual documentation** and lack of real-time tracking tools.
* **Procurement corruption** and material price inflation.
* **Supply chain bottlenecks** that cause delayed deliveries and waste.

---

## 💡 Problem Statement

> How might we build a unified platform that enhances **transparency, accountability, and coordination** in construction projects — by integrating cost management tools with smart supply chain tracking?

---

## 🌐 Solution Overview

**ConSync** is a centralized platform where **clients, contractors, and suppliers** can plan, track, and manage construction projects collaboratively in real time — from budgeting to material delivery.

---

## 🔩 Core Modules

### 🧱 Project & Cost Management

* Project dashboard (milestones, deadlines, budgets)
* Expense tracker (budget vs actuals, visual analytics)
* Document management (BOQs, drawings, contracts)
* AI-driven alerts for project delays or cost overruns
* Real-time client–contractor updates

---

### 🚚 Supply Chain & Logistics

* Verified supplier marketplace
* Track-and-trace system for material delivery
* Price monitoring across regions
* Procurement request system with order tracking
* Delivery verification via QR or photo proof

---

### 🤝 Collaboration & Transparency

* Role-based access: Client, Contractor, Supplier, Project Engineer
* In-app messaging + progress logs
* Full audit trail for all actions

---

### 📊 Analytics & Insights

* Cost and timeline performance charts
* Supplier reliability analytics
* Project-level reports (PDF/Excel export)

## 🧭 Example Use Case

A local contractor managing a 12-flat project in Enugu uses **ConSync** to:

* Input milestones and budgets.
* Order materials from verified suppliers.
* Track delivery status.
* Share progress with the client in real time.
  **Result:** Less delay, zero hidden costs, and full accountability.

## 🧰 Tech Stack

**Frontend:** React (Vite + Tailwind CSS, JavaScript preferred)
**Backend:** Node.js + Express
**Database:** MongoDB (or PostgreSQL if relational structure is needed)
**Integrations:**

* Google Maps API (supplier location tracking)
* EmailJS / Twilio (notifications)
* Recharts / Chart.js (data visualization)

**Deployment:**

* Frontend → Vercel
* Backend → Render / Railway
* Database → MongoDB Atlas

---

## 🔐 Authentication & Roles

* Role system: Client, Contractor, Supplier, Project Engineer
* Role-based dashboards and permissions
